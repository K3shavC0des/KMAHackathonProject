What is
SARView?

SARView helps farmers and crop advisers choose where to check soil moisture before making irrigation decisions.

About the project

The map compares surface moisture estimates from NASA and ISRO's NISAR satellite across two dates, helping identify areas to inspect in the field.

Choose a city, zoom in, and select a square to see its readings and how they changed.

The tour takes about 90 seconds.

Crop + weather watch

How this reading is combined

Weather adds city-level context. Only NISAR moisture change colors the map blocks. Crop guidance suggests possible crops; it does not verify what is planted or change the signal.

Green: lower concern, median fell under 2 points or rose
Yellow: watch, median fell 2–3.5 points
Red: review sooner, median fell 3.5+ points

The weather screen covers the city. Each roughly 1 km block summarizes at least 10 measured cells and can contain gaps. Crop and growth stage are unknown; these colours are inspection cues, not confirmed crop loss.

What the colours mean
