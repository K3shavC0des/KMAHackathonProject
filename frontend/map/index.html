What is
NISAR?

A satellite view of surface moisture change.

The short version

NISAR is a satellite built by NASA and India's space agency, ISRO. Its radar observes Earth through clouds and at night.

This map compares its estimates of moisture in the top layer of soil on two dates. A drop can point to a place worth checking in person. It does not tell you to water.

The tour takes about 90 seconds. Pause or skip whenever you want.
