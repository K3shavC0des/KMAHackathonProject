# Project overview

Farm Moisture Watch is a map-based prototype for helping a farmer or crop adviser choose an area to inspect first for possible surface drying. It compares two quality-screened NISAR soil-moisture observations across the same analysis squares and makes the dates, measurements, and source details visible.

## What the map shows

The map includes observed data views for Nagapattinam, Ludhiana, and Ahmedabad, along with a separately labeled synthetic example. Users can select a square to inspect its later-minus-earlier moisture change and the evidence supporting it. Rankings are based on the available local NISAR signal and are intended to guide inspection, not prescribe irrigation.

The displayed shapes are analysis squares, not surveyed farm boundaries or verified crop areas. NISAR surface moisture is not root-zone moisture, crop stress, or proof that irrigation is needed. The observations are historical, and local conditions should be checked before making water-management decisions.

## Data limits

Regional SMAP historical context and matching L/S-band radar results are shown as unavailable unless suitable quality-checked inputs are available. The project does not fill missing evidence with estimates or claim measured water savings, yield gains, or farmer outcomes.
